🎯 Key Metrics Explained

MRR — Monthly Recurring Revenue. Your predictable monthly income from subscriptions.
ARR — Annual Recurring Revenue. MRR × 12. The key metric investors look at.
LTV — Lifetime Value. Total revenue per customer = ARPU / Churn Rate.
Churn — % of customers lost per month. Keep below 5% for healthy growth.
