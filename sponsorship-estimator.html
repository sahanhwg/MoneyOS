📊 Pricing Formula

Our estimator uses the industry-standard formula:

Rate = Base × Niche × Engagement

Where base rate is determined by your follower tier, niche multiplier ranges from 0.8x to 2.5x, and engagement multiplier adjusts for above/below-average interaction rates.

🎯 Negotiation Tips

1. Lead with engagement
A 5% engagement rate justifies 2x the standard rate for your tier.
2. Bundle for value
Offer packages (feed + stories + reels) at 15-20% discount for volume.
3. Include usage rights
Charge 25-50% more if the brand wants to reuse your content in ads.
