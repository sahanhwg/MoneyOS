🎯 Pricing Strategies

Value-Based Pricing
Charge based on the value you create, not your time. Clients pay for outcomes.
Retainer Model
Monthly retainers provide predictable income. Add 10-15% premium for exclusivity.
Project-Based
Fixed-price projects eliminate scope creep. Always include 20% buffer for revisions.
