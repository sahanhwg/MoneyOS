📊 Break-Even Formula

BE = Fixed Costs / (Price - Variable Cost)

Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost
This is the amount each unit contributes toward covering fixed costs.

Margin Ratio = CM / Price × 100
Shows what percentage of each sale is profit after variable costs.

🎯 Strategies to Lower Break-Even

1. Reduce fixed costs
Negotiate rent, use shared workspaces, automate processes to cut overhead.
2. Lower variable costs
Bulk purchasing, better supplier terms, or manufacturing improvements.
3. Increase price
Premium positioning can increase contribution margin without losing significant volume.
